About Roadway Solar

Covering solar pavement and road-integrated PV — from walkable panels and pavement tiles to the canopies and energy pavers that pave the way.

OUR STORY

Roads at the Frontier of Energy

Roadway Solar covers the full spectrum of additive manufacturing for photovoltaics — from walkable road panels and pavement tiles to the canopies, energy pavers, and embedded electronics that make up road-integrated PV.

Our editorial team includes civil engineers, developers, and materials scientists with real project experience across roads, walkways, and parking, weatherable polymers, and design-to-fabrication workflows.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To cover solar AM from the printed contact finger to the full mounting system with equal depth — the cell, the hardware, and the material behind them.

Our Vision

To be the authoritative, vendor-independent source for additive manufacturing in solar — connecting installers and developers to the suppliers reshaping how PV hardware gets made.
